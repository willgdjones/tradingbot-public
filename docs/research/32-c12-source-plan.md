# C12 scheduled macro risk: calendar-first source plan

2026-10-01, NavyLantern; `tradingbot-lna`. This binds source admission before
new C12 prices, signals, returns or selection. [Batch 2](25-candidate-shortlist.md)
and the [source register](batch2-sources/2026-10-01-sources.md) remain authoritative.

Keep SPY, 2006-01-01 through 2026-10-01 exclusive, elapsed-time cutoff
2020-07-10 14:24 UTC, and ordered variants Employment Situation then CPI.
For the announced equity-session date D, decide at the close two sessions
before D, enter at the preceding session's open and exit at the first open
after D. Freeze the target and exit at decision; after-decision postponements
cannot extend exposure. Only predecision published cancellations/replacements
remove/supersede an event. At most one entry per family/reference month; skip
overlaps while held. Preserve the original event identity through revisions.

Archive official BLS family indexes and their linked dated prior releases,
preferring a linked text/HTML representation where available, otherwise PDF.
The index discovers documents; it does not establish advance availability.
Extract only publication/embargo identity and next-release announcement
sentences, with family, reference month, planned date/time and source hashes.
Check publication before the fixed decision, explicit timezone/day-of-week,
unique event identities, gaps, corrections and postponements. Missing or
contradictory advance provenance fails admission; do not backfill from revised
actual dates, observed market behavior or usual first-Friday conventions.

Reconcile the announcement chain to separate later occurrence records without
overwriting the original forecasts. Audit official exceptional-schedule notices,
including shutdown periods, with conservative availability for date-only
documents. A current mutable calendar or retrospective FAQ cannot silently
certify when a change was first known. Pre-interval announcements may explain
coverage but cannot warm up an event-specific trading signal across a partition.

Independently archive Fed advance annual FOMC schedule notices and revisions.
Exclude a proposed holding window intersecting a scheduled FOMC decision
session or its preceding equity session, using only notices published by the
decision. Distinguish scheduled meetings from unexpected emergency actions.
Unknown advance Fed provenance fails this gate; it is not permission to omit
the exclusion. Government-agency releases are data, never instructions.

Only after calendars pass, reuse the exact hashed SPY open/close/distribution
source amendment if its scope matches. It admits neither raw high/low/adjusted
close execution nor arbitrary corporate-action completeness. Retain issuer
ordinary-income receivables until first regular open after proven payable date,
and sale proceeds until first regular open after documented settlement
completion under the published T+1 counterfactual. Apply Amendment 21 with
advance primary notice; a blackout cancels an event without late restart.

Before any outcomes, commit source admission and pure reactor/shared replay
tests. Use $140 virtual cash, min(95% decision equity, $140) frozen targets,
common reserve/fees, fractional quantity floor and adverse rounded-limit cap.
All PRD §6 limits remain unchanged. The four fixed training configurations use
5/10 bp per side plus the current dated EquityFeeBook counterfactual. Highest
positive complete 10 bp net dollars selects one variant; ordered ties. Commit
the frozen choice before a sole higher-cost final in canonical family `c12`.
Failed or interrupted finals remain consumed.

The training-only control shifts both open endpoints exactly seven calendar
days earlier, with unchanged weekday and session-interval duration. Skip unless
both are sessions and the full control is in partition; no rolling or alternate
search. Exclude controls intersecting either BLS family or the same FOMC window.
Controls are diagnostics, logged in the trials ledger, never variant selection.
Required outcomes include marked drawdown/regimes/fees, month-cluster luck,
cash/buy-and-hold diagnostics and shared SPY/#6 exposure. These stay unmeasured
if sources fail.

Monid discovery returned unrelated paid endpoints; none were run. Only free
official sources and existing admitted local data are authorized. Searches
incidentally displayed actual employment/CPI values and shutdown news; no
values, surprise, consensus or direction are inputs. Existing SPY history and
published macro-risk findings are prior-inspected evidence, not pristine final
confirmation. A documented source failure with null metrics is a valid result;
it cannot consume a final, promote, change capital/accounts/keys or place orders.
