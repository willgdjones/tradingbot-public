## Option/account mechanics and the actual data gate

- [Alpaca historical option data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/historical-option-data): history from February 2024; free indicative quotes are modified derivatives and derived trades are delayed. This does not establish executable past BBO.
- [Alpaca options overview](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading-overview): cash-secured puts and purchased calls/puts have distinct approval/mechanics; contracts are indivisible. Existing account permission must be inspected, never changed by this task.
- [Alpaca regulatory fees](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/regulatory-fees): future simulator must freeze all applicable option fees and rounding; no exact fee table has been fabricated in this desk review.
- [OIC long straddle](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/long-straddle): same-strike call/put exposure can lose both premiums; implied volatility, time and exercise matter.
- [OIC cash-secured put](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/cash-secured-put): full strike obligation and stock downside remain despite cash collateral.
- Local [harness evidence](../10-options-data-harness.md) and its [audit](../10-options-data-harness-results.json) establish exactly what existing probes contain and lack. No new quote/bar probe was performed here. Today's F/SPY examples cannot establish C13/C14 historical chains or affordability.

## Rejected shortcuts and additional selection exposure

- [CFTC historical HTML](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/HistoricalViewable/index.htm) and [revision announcements](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/HistoricalSpecialAnnouncements/index.htm) expressly document historical revisions/reposting. HTML is not automatically original-vintage evidence.
- COT's economic case is mixed: [de Roon–Nijman–Veld](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/0022-1082.00253) versus [Gorton–Hayashi–Rouwenhorst](https://www.nber.org/papers/w13249). Neither supplies a validated GLD threshold.
- [Heston et al., Option Momentum](https://faculty.marshall.usc.edu/Christopher-Jones/pdf/opmom.pdf) reports that full-spread costs can remove apparent returns. Do not add a costly, long-warm-up strategy merely because option bars exist.
- Pre-holiday effects have original [Ariel evidence](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1990.tb03731.x) and [later decline/reversal evidence](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0261560605000938); no extra candidate is proposed.
