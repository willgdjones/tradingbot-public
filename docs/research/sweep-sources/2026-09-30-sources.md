# Strategy-sweep source register

Retrieved/reviewed 2026-09-30 Pacific / 2026-10-01 UTC. These are short original
desk-review notes plus durable source identifiers, not copies of copyrighted
papers. No source portfolio returns were adopted as bot returns. No candidate
price series was inspected. Monid discovery was used; its discovered paid
endpoints were not run. Primary author, journal, regulator and provider sources
were used through free web access. Some NBER/EIA fetches were unavailable on
repeat access; indexed primary abstracts/author copies and the documented
archive structure support the notes, not a completed data-quality audit.

| Key | Primary source | What it supports / limit |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | [Faber, A Quantitative Approach to Tactical Asset Allocation](https://mebfaber.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/SSRN-id962461.pdf), pp. 20–21 | Monthly ten-month moving-average design. Published index/close/cash assumptions differ from the proposed ETF execution. |
| S02 | [Faber, Relative Strength Strategies for Investing](https://mebfaber.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/SSRN-id1585517-Relative-Strength-Strategies-for-Investing.pdf), pp. 5–6, 12–17 | Sector and asset-class ranking/top-N rotation, composite horizons and optional SMA filter. Fixed ETF universes here are adaptations. |
| S03 | [Moskowitz and Grinblatt, Do Industries Explain Momentum?](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/0022-1082.00146), DOI 10.1111/0022-1082.00146 | Industry momentum evidence, not proof for a long-only nine-ETF portfolio. |
| S04 | [Moreira and Muir, Volatility-Managed Portfolios](https://law.yale.edu/sites/default/files/area/workshop/leo/leo17_moreira.pdf), §II.C; [NBER w22208](https://www.nber.org/papers/w22208) | Inverse-variance allocation and constrained leverage cases. Fixed c/95% cap here is an ex-ante adaptation. |
| S05 | [Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen, Time Series Momentum](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Time-Series-Momentum) | Own-return persistence across futures; does not validate ETF roll exposure or long-only profitability. |

The proposed underweight long-side/month-end ETF proxy needs its own test. |
| S07 | [Nagel, Evaporating Liquidity](https://www.nber.org/papers/w17653), DOI 10.3386/w17653 | Short-term reversals as liquidity provision, varying with stress. Unconditional long-only ETF reversal is not an established result. |
| S08 | [Gorton, Hayashi and Rouwenhorst, The Fundamentals of Commodity Futures Returns](https://www.nber.org/papers/w13249), DOI 10.3386/w13249 | Inventory-related futures risk premia. Our year-ago USO signal is a testable proxy, not replication. |

An affordable premium is insufficient execution evidence. |
